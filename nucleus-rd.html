R&D requests arrive from customers through Scinode Customers →. In Nucleus, the internal team plans the work, runs it, and tracks it end to end, with the cost visible at every stage.

The workflow
Plan it, run it, track it.
01
Plan
Every request starts with a plan: the route, a paper costing, where the work runs from lab to scale-up, and the QC and regulatory path.
02
Execute
The plan becomes live work, and experiments are run against it.
03
Track
Experimentation is tracked end to end, so the team can see how the work is going and what it is costing.
Route selection→Paper costing→Lab→Plant selection to scale-up→QC→Regulatory→Execution→Tracking
The key decision
Budgeted cost and actual cost, tracked together.

Paper costing sets the budget for a project before any experiment runs. Experiment tracking measures what the work actually costs. Putting the two side by side makes the cost burn visible against the budget.
