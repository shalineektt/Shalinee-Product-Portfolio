Product decisions
Two decisions that keep the three sides in step.
→One proposal, many versions. A proposal stays one complete proposal through negotiation. Every change a partner or the business team submits is saved as a new version, so nothing is overwritten and the history stays clear.
→One project ID across all three sides. The customer, the business team and the partner platform follow the same project, so progress means the same thing to everyone.
What I owned
→The opportunity workflow, from a customer request to opportunities sent to partners, including bulk upload for the business team.
→Proposal management and negotiation: one proposal across its life, with versions, from submission to project created.
→Project tracking that ties one project together across Scinode Customers, Scinode Admin and Scinode for Researchers.
