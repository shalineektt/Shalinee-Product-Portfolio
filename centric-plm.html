My scope
I expedited the workflows behind Reliance Retail’s fashion and apparel business.

Centric PLM is Centric Software’s product lifecycle platform for fashion and retail. Kripya implemented it for Reliance Retail, and I worked with Reliance’s business teams to configure workflows around how each brand develops and sources its products. About Centric PLM ↗

The challenge
One platform, many brands, many product types.

A dress, a jumpsuit and a shirt need different details. Each brand records them its own way, and sourcing needs them complete and consistent before a supplier can quote.

Many brands
Avasa, Avantakrita and Reliance’s other apparel brands each develop products differently.
Many product types
Each apparel type needs its own BOM detail, captured in a shared structure.
Many suppliers
Quotes had to be kept against each product, so they could be compared like for like.

The chain
From a style to a purchase order.

Centric PLM holds the whole chain on one product record. I worked on its middle: the BOM that makes a style sourceable, and the suppliers and quotes that follow it.

What I built
Four workflows, one chain.
01
BOM workflows
A workflow to capture the bill of materials for every brand and apparel type, such as Avasa and Avantakrita, in one structure.
02
Sourcing support
BOM details captured in the PLM feed sourcing, so every supplier request starts from the same product record.
03
Supplier onboarding
Reliance Retail’s suppliers onboarded to the platform, so requests, responses and quotes happen in one place.
04
Quote management
Supplier quotes maintained for each product, with their state, so the team compares like with like.
Complete BOMs in, comparable quotes out.
Quotes
Quotes stay with the product they were for.

A supplier answers a request on the platform, and the quote is held as pending until it is checked. If details are missing, it goes back with a comment. Once complete, it is approved and selected for its colourway group.

Inside the platform
The product record, in use.

What to read in it

  1. 01StateEach quote is approved or pending, so the team sees what is ready to select.
  2. 02CommentA comment records what is missing, such as a material list, before the quote goes back.
